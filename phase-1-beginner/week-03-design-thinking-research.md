# Week 03: Design Thinking & Research

This week is all about putting Design Thinking into practice !

You will explore how designers understand users, identify real problems, and develop meaningful opportunities through research.

## Learning Goals

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the first three stages of Design Thinking.
- Recognize the importance of user research.
- Distinguish between qualitative and quantitative research.
- Prepare for and conduct user interviews.
- Write effective survey questions.
- Identify users' needs, behaviors, and pain points.
- Create a User Persona based on research findings.
- Build an Empathy Map.
- Write a clear Problem Statement.
- Formulate How Might We (HMW) questions.
- Generate ideas based on real user needs.

### Task

1. Watch all the assigned videos in order, and write a short summary of each.
2. Pay special attention to the **third video about Doug Dietz**. Understand his story, note what inspired you, and write a short reflection on his approach.
3. Choose a real-world problem to investigate, and identify its target audience.
4. Conduct at least **2 user interviews** to understand users' needs, experiences, and pain points.
5. If needed, create a **User Survey** to gather additional insights from a wider audience.
6. Create a **User Persona** based on your research findings.
7. Build an **Empathy Map** to capture what users Say, Think, Do, and Feel.
8. Write a clear **Problem Statement** based on your research.
9. Create at least **3 How Might We (HMW) Questions** to explore opportunities for solving the problem.
