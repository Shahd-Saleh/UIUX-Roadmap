# Week 02: UX Fundamentals

## 1. Week Overview

This week, you'll go deeper into **User Experience** and learn how designers approach problems from the user's perspective.

You'll explore the fundamentals of UX Design, see why User-Centered Design matters, and discover how the Design Thinking process leads to meaningful solutions

The goal is to **stop thinking only about how a product looks, and start thinking about how it works, why it exists, and how it solves users' problems.**

## 2. Learning Goals

By the end of this week, you'll be able to:

- Explain the core principles of User Experience Design.
- Explain why usability and user satisfaction matter.
- Use a structured problem-solving approach to everyday problems.
