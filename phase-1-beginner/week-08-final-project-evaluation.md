# Week 08: Final Project & Evaluation

It's time to bring everything you've learned together and work on your first complete UX/UI project!

Your challenge is to identify a real-world problem, understand the people facing it, and design a digital solution that addresses their needs.

### Tasks

1. **Choose a Real Problem:** Identify a real-world problem that you believe could be solved or improved through a digital product.
2. **Apply Design Thinking:** Follow the Design Thinking process to understand the problem, explore possible solutions, and define your idea.
3. **Conduct UX Research:** Identify your target users, conduct user interviews, and gather insights about their needs and challenges.
4.  **Prepare the essential UX deliverables:**
    - User Persona
    - Empathy Map
    - Problem Statement
    - How Might We (HMW) Questions
    - Basic User Flow
5. **Create Wireframes:** Sketch your main screens on paper or create low-fidelity wireframes directly in Figma.
6. **Design the UI:** Turn your wireframes into polished UI screens, applying the design principles you've learned.
7. **Build an Interactive Prototype:** Connect your screens in Figma, add interactions, and create a prototype that demonstrates the main user journey.
8. **Present Your Project:** Organize your work and prepare a short presentation explaining the problem, your design process, and your final solution.

## 🎉 Congratulations on completing the Beginner Level!

You've made it through your first step in the UI/UX journey.

Remember, great designers aren't made in a day. Every project, every mistake, and every challenge is part of your growth.

Give your final project your best effort, be creative, trust your ideas, and don't be afraid to experiment.

**You're no longer just learning about design; you're starting to think and work like a designer.**

Keep going !
