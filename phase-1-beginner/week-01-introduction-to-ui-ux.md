# Week 01: Introduction to UI/UX

**Welcome to your first step into the world of UI/UX Design!**

In this week, you'll discover what UI/UX Design is, why it matters, and how designers create digital experiences that are both useful and enjoyable.

You'll explore the difference between UI and UX, understand the role of a UI/UX Designer, and start looking at everyday digital products from a designer's perspective.

**The goal is to start thinking like a designer, not just looking at designs.**

## Learning Goals

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand what UI Design and UX Design mean.
- Identify the differences between UI and UX.
- Understand the role and responsibilities of a UI/UX Designer.
- Explore the basic UI/UX design process
