# Week 24, 25: Final Product Project & Evaluation

In this final project, you will work as part of a cross-functional product team to transform a real-world problem into a functional digital product.

You will collaborate with other designers and developers, experience a realistic product development workflow, communicate across different disciplines, and take your product from the initial idea to final implementation and evaluation.

**This is a team project, not an individual design assignment.**

## **Project Phases**

**Phase 1 : Team Formation & Project Discovery**

- Form a cross-functional team consisting of Product Designers and Developers.
- Define team roles and responsibilities.
- Identify a real-world problem worth solving.
- Define the target audience and product scope.
- Establish the product vision and objectives.

**Deliverables:**

- Team Structure & Roles
- Problem Definition
- Product Vision & Goals
- Project Scope

**Phase 2 : Research & Product Strategy**

- Conduct market and competitor research.
- Define research objectives and methods.
- Gather and analyze user research data.
- Identify user needs, behaviors, and pain points.
- Develop a Value Proposition.
- Define product priorities and key features.

**Deliverables:**

- Research Plan & Findings
- Competitor Analysis
- User Insights
- Value Proposition
- Prioritized Feature List

**Phase 3 : UX Design & Product Structure**

- Define the product's Information Architecture.
- Create User Flows for key scenarios.
- Develop low-fidelity wireframes.
- Define the main user interactions.
- Review and validate the proposed experience with the team.

**Deliverables:**

- Information Architecture
- User Flows
- Low-Fidelity Wireframes
- UX Design Decisions

**Phase 4 : Visual Design & Design System**

- Explore visual directions and establish the product's art direction.
- Create a consistent visual identity for the product.
- Build a scalable Design System in Figma.
- Define colors, typography, spacing, and design tokens.
- Create reusable components and their states.
- Design high-fidelity UI screens.

**Deliverables:**

- Visual Direction
- Design System
- Component Library
- High-Fidelity UI Screens

**Phase 5 : Responsive Design & Interactive Prototyping**

- Adapt the product interfaces for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Build interactive components and realistic user scenarios.
- Implement advanced interactions and meaningful animations.
- Create a high-fidelity interactive prototype.
- Review the experience with the team.

**Deliverables:**

- Responsive UI Designs
- Interactive Components
- Animated Prototype
- Complete User Scenarios

**Phase 6 : Developer Handoff & Implementation**

- Organize and prepare Figma files for development.
- Document design specifications, components, and interaction behaviors.
- Prepare assets and development-ready resources.
- Communicate design decisions and requirements to developers.
- Collaborate with developers throughout implementation.
- Review the implemented product against the original designs.

**Deliverables:**

- Developer-Ready Figma File
- Design Specifications & Assets
- Handoff Documentation
- Implemented Product

**Phase 7 : Testing, Evaluation & Optimization**

- Test the implemented product across different devices and screen sizes.
- Identify UI inconsistencies and usability issues.
- Evaluate key user scenarios.
- Collect feedback and analyze findings.
- Define relevant product success metrics.
- Identify improvements and refine the final product.

**Deliverables:**

- Testing Results
- Usability Findings
- UI Implementation Review
- Product Evaluation
- Improvement Recommendations

**Phase 8 : Final Presentation & Project Showcase**

- Prepare a complete presentation of the project.
- Explain the problem, research, strategy, and design decisions.
- Demonstrate the final functional product.
- Showcase the collaboration between designers and developers.
- Present challenges, iterations, and lessons learned.
- Document the entire process as a professional case study.

**Deliverables:**

- Final Product Demo
- Complete Project Presentation
- Professional UX/UI Case Study
- Team Retrospective

**Final Project Requirements**

- The project must be developed by a cross-functional team, not as an individual assignment.
- The product must solve a clearly defined real-world problem.
- Designers and developers must collaborate throughout the project.
- The final outcome must be a functional product, not just a Figma prototype.
- The team must document its process, decisions, and iterations.
- Each team member must have a clearly defined role and demonstrate their contribution.

**The Journey Doesn't End Here !**

Don't focus on becoming a perfect designer. Focus on becoming a better one every day.

**Keep learning , Keep creating , Keep growing !**

And most importantly, never stop building things that make a difference !
