# Week 16: UX Writing & Usability Evaluation

This week, you will focus on improving the clarity and usability of your product through **UX Writing and Usability Evaluation**. You will learn how to communicate effectively with users through clear, meaningful interface content, and how to evaluate your design by testing it with real users, identifying usability issues, and making improvements based on their feedback.

By the end of this week, you should be able to create a more understandable, user-friendly, and effective experience.

### Task

1. Review your existing screens and improve unclear UI text.
2. Write microcopy for at least 3 UI elements (e.g. buttons, error messages, or empty states).
3. Test your prototype with 2–3 users by asking them to complete one simple task.
4. Identify 2–3 usability issues based on their feedback.
5. Make improvements to your design based on your findings.
