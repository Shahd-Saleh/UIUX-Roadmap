# Week 17, 18: Final Project & Evaluation

This is your opportunity to put everything you've learned throughout the Intermediate Level into practice.

You will start a completely new digital product project from scratch, taking it through the entire UX/UI design process , from identifying a real-world problem and conducting user research to creating a complete, responsive, and interactive digital experience.

You are expected to apply your knowledge of UX Research, Information Architecture, UX Writing, Visual Design, Responsive Design, Figma, Animation, Prototyping, and Design Systems.

Your goal is not simply to create beautiful screens, but to demonstrate your ability to think like a designer, solve real user problems, and build a meaningful digital product from idea to final prototype.

By the end of this challenge, you should have a complete, professionally presented UX/UI case study that demonstrates your readiness to take on real-world design challenges.

### Task

Design a completely new digital product that solves a real problem for a specific target audience.

You can choose a Web App, Mobile App, or Responsive Website, depending on the problem you're solving.

The project must demonstrate the complete UX/UI design process.

### Phase 1  : Product Discovery & Research

**Goal:** Understand the problem, the users, and the market.

**Deliverables:**

- Product Brief
- Problem Definition
- Research Plan
- Interview Questions / Survey
- Research Findings
- Competitor Analysis
- Feature Comparison Matrix

### Phase 2 : Research Synthesis & User Understanding

**Goal:** Turn research data into meaningful user insights.

**Deliverables:**

- Affinity Map
- Key User Insights
- User Personas
- Empathy Map
- User Journey Map
- Problem Statement
- How Might We Questions

### Phase 3 : Information Architecture & UX Design

**Goal:** Structure the product and create intuitive user experiences.

**Deliverables:**

- Information Architecture
- User Flows
- UX Improvements

### Phase 4 : Visual Research & **Design System**

**Goal:** Develop a strong visual identity and translate it into polished interfaces.

**Deliverables:**

- Color & Typography Styles
- Spacing Guidelines
- Reusable Components
- Component Variants & States
- Organized Design System

### Phase 5 : UI Screens Design & Prototype

**Deliverables:**

- Responsive Figma Layouts
- High-Fidelity Interactive Prototype
- Animate Effects
- Animated UI Elements
- Complete Interactive User Journey
- Tested & Refined Prototype

### Phase 6 : Final Presentation & Complete UX/UI Case Study

**Goal:** Present your entire design process in a professional case study that demonstrates your problem-solving skills, design decisions, and final product.

**Important Note:** Your case study should not simply showcase the final screens. It should tell the complete story of your project , the problem you identified, the research you conducted, the decisions you made, and how your final solution addresses users' needs.

Great designers don't just create great products; they know how to explain the thinking behind them.

### A Final Piece of Advice

Never stop seeking feedback.

One of the most important steps in your growth as a designer is finding someone more experienced than you , a mentor, senior designer, or professional in the field ,who can review your work, challenge your decisions, and give you honest, constructive feedback.

Don't rely only on your own judgment. Sometimes, you need someone with a more experienced eye to notice things you might have missed and help you see your work from a different perspective.

**Seek feedback, stay open to criticism, and never be afraid to improve.**

Remember, feedback isn't a judgment of your abilities; it's an opportunity to become better.
