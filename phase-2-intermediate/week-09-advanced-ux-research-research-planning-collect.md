# Week 09: Advanced UX Research & Research Planning (Collect)

This week focuses on learning how to plan and conduct structured UX research, gather reliable user data, and understand the competitive landscape.

You will explore different research methods, learn how to choose the right approach for your research goals, and evaluate existing products to understand their features and market positioning.

The goal is to develop a research-driven mindset and build a solid foundation for the upcoming stages of your product project

## Learning Goals

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the main types and purposes of UX research.
- Define clear research goals.
- Select appropriate research methods.
- Prepare and conduct structured user interviews and surveys.
- Recognize common research biases and avoid leading questions.
- Identify and evaluate direct and indirect competitors.
- Compare competing products using structured frameworks.
- Identify market gaps and opportunities.

Task

Choose a new digital product idea that addresses a real-world problem.

Your goal is to conduct preliminary user research and competitive analysis before moving into the design process.

**Part 1  : Research Planning**

1. Define your product idea and the problem it aims to address.
2. Identify your potential target audience.
3. Write 3–5 research objectives.
4. Choose suitable research methods and explain why you selected them.

**Part 2 : User Research**

5. Prepare user interview questions and/or a survey.

6. Conduct 5–8 user interviews or collect a suitable number of survey responses.

7. Document the responses and key observations without jumping to conclusions.

**Part 3 : Competitive Analysis**

8. Identify at least 3 direct or indirect competitors.

9. Evaluate their features, strengths, weaknesses, and overall experience.

10. Create a Feature Comparison Matrix.

11. Identify at least 3 potential market gaps or opportunities.
