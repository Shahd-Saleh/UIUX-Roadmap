# Week 12: Design Systems

This week, you will learn how to build and organize a Design System that ensures consistency across your product. You will explore the core elements of a design system, including design tokens, typography, colors, spacing, and reusable components.

You will also learn how to create, document, and maintain reusable UI components, turning your individual screens into a consistent and scalable design experience.

By the end of this week, you should be able to build a basic Design System for your product and apply it consistently across your interfaces.

### Task

1. Create a color palette and define your typography styles.
2. Establish a consistent spacing and sizing system.
3. Create reusable UI components (e.g. Buttons, Inputs, Cards, and Navigation).
4. Define component variants and different states.
5. Organize your components into a basic Design System in Figma.
6. Apply your Design System to your existing product screens.
