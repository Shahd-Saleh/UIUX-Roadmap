# Week 11: Visual Design & Art Direction

This week is all about developing your visual eye and learning how to create visually appealing and polished interfaces. You will explore different visual styles, study inspiring designs, understand what makes them visually effective, and learn how to translate visual inspiration into your own design direction.

The goal is not to copy beautiful designs, but to understand the thinking behind them and develop your own visual design approach.

## Week Goals

By the end of this week, learners should be able to:

1. Develop a stronger visual sense and recognize high-quality UI designs.
2. Find and collect relevant visual inspiration.
3. Analyze design references and understand the decisions behind them.
4. Explore different visual styles and art directions.
5. Create moodboards and translate inspiration into visual concepts.
6. Make more intentional visual design decisions.

### Task

1. Collect **10–15 visual references** related to your chosen screen.
2. Create a Moodboard that represents your preferred visual direction.
3. Analyze at least 3 references: What makes their visual design effective?
4. Explore **2 different Visual Directions** for your chosen screen.
5. Choose one direction and explain why it fits your product.
