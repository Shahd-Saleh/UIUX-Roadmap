# 🎨 UI/UX Roadmap 2026

> A 25-week, project-based roadmap that takes you from zero to a job-ready UI/UX Designer — Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced.

**[📚 Resources](RESOURCES.md)** · Phase 1: Weeks 1–8 · Phase 2: Weeks 9–18 · Phase 3: Weeks 19–25

---

## 💬 A Message Before You Start

First, remember that there is no such thing as a perfect or fully comprehensive roadmap that covers everything in UI/UX Design.

This roadmap is simply a guide to help you get started, build your foundations, and know what to learn next. It is not a limitation on your learning journey.

You are encouraged to explore beyond the provided resources, do your own research, practice independently, and keep learning. The world of design is constantly evolving, and learning resources are endless.

**Take ownership of your learning journey. Be curious, explore, experiment, and never stop improving.**
